Regulation Breach Analyzer

Describe a security incident. Open-source AI models — downloaded once and run entirely on your device — figure out which data-protection regulations were likely triggered, and explain specifically how each one was breached.

How this works

Two small open-source models run back to back, entirely on your device, via Transformers.js:

  1. Matching — a zero-shot classifier (DeBERTa-v3-xsmall, fine-tuned on MNLI) scores how well your incident entails each candidate regulation, without ever being trained on breach examples specifically.
  2. Explaining — for every regulation that scores high enough, a small instruction-tuned generative model (LaMini-Flan-T5-248M) writes a sentence explaining, in its own words, exactly how that regulation appears to have been violated.

Both models download once (cached by your browser) and everything afterward runs instantly, offline, and privately — your incident text never leaves your device.

Educational demo only — not legal advice. Explanations are generated by a small open-source language model and can be wrong or oversimplified.
